K.I.N.D. Fund Scholarships Build Brighter Futures in Malawi
unicef usa — 1 Oct 2026

As young learners head back to school this fall, The K.I.N.D. (Kids in Need of Desks) Fund will be making an impact in Malawi, continuing its 16-year mission to outfit classrooms with desks and provide scholarships to help students like Joyce Chisale fulfill their dreams.

In her first year at St. Michaels Girls Secondary School in Mangochi, Malawi, Joyce Chisale got the highest marks in everything from Chichewa language classes to biology and history. But her parents had trouble keeping up with payments; the boarding school's annual fees were roughly half her father's yearly salary, and there were three other siblings to support.

That might have meant the abrupt end of Joyce's formal education and her dreams of becoming a doctor or scientist. Then The K.I.N.D. (Kids in Need of Desks) Fund stepped in, awarding Joyce a full, merit-based scholarship. She went on to complete high school and graduated at the top of her class.

Helios and UNICEF aim to enhance digital learning access in Africa
Developing Telecoms — 29 Sep 2026

Helios Towers, a telecommunications infrastructure company, and child rights organisation UNICEF (the United Nations Children’s Fund) have announced a multi-year partnership to expand internet connectivity and digital learning access for children and young people across Africa.

The partnership supports two flagship UNICEF initiatives: Giga, a partnership between UNICEF and the International Telecommunication Union working to connect every school in the world to the internet by 2030, and Learning Passport, which provides localised, high-quality digital learning and skills for children, young people and teachers to continue learning anywhere, at any time, both online and offline.

The partnership will combine Helios Towers’ telecommunications infrastructure expertise with UNICEF’s global leadership in digital inclusion to help connect schools and expand access to quality education in underserved communities.

Did UNICEF predict Niger State will not have boys in Grade 7 by 2027?
Dubawa — 1 Oct 2026

Claim: Niger State Governor Umaru Bago recently claimed a UNICEF report predicted that by 2027, there would be no male child in Grade 7/JSS1 in Shiroro and Munya local government areas of the state because children had left school and gone into artisanal mining.

Verdict: False. UNICEF told DUBAWA it did not conduct or commission a study on the impact of artisanal mining on children’s school attendance in Shiroro or other Local Government Areas (LGAs) in Niger State.

UNESCO collaborates with Tanzania National Commission to promote youth skills and innovation
UNESCO — 28 Sep 2026

Young people from across Tanzania showcased their practical skills, creativity and innovation at the WorldSkills Tanzania National Skills Competition 2026, which received support from the UNESCO Dar es Salaam Office. The competition formed part of the National Education, Skills and Innovation Week, organized by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. During the week, UNESCO collaborated with the Tanzania National Commission for UNESCO (NATCOM) to showcase their programmes and activities.

The competition was organized by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology in collaboration with the National Council for Technical and Vocational Education and Training (NACTVET) and other education and skills-development stakeholders.

The week concluded on 24 August 2026 with a closing ceremony officiated by the Prime Minister of the United Republic of Tanzania, Hon. Dr Mwigulu Lameck Nchemba, at the grounds of Usagara Secondary School in Tanga.
