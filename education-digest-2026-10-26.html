New Catholic University of Eastern Africa Chancellor Calls for Renewed Evangelization through Education in AI Era
ACI Africa — 23 Oct 2026

The newly installed Chancellor of the Catholic University of Eastern Africa (CUEA) in Kenya has called for the institution to sustain its mission of evangelization through education amid the growth of social media and artificial intelligence (AI).

Speaking on Thursday, October 22, following his installation, Archbishop Gervas John Mwasikwabhila Nyaisonga of Tanzania’s Catholic Archdiocese of Mbeya said CUEA’s responsibility was to carry forward the mission entrusted to it by its founders.

“Our task is to ensure that this legacy of evangelization through education and formation of our students continues and is as effective today in the era of social media and artificial intelligence as it was over 40 years ago,” Archbishop Nyaisonga said at the end of the Holy Mass preceding CUEA’s October 23 graduation.
