Liberia's Cash Aid Program Improves Finances but Raises Partner Conflict
SuaraGarut.ID — 24 Sep 2026

In 2022, over 2,300 households in Maryland and Bomi counties, Liberia, received $750 in cash transfers split into four payments, giving both partners equal funds and decision-making power, according to a study released in July.

The program aimed to increase family income and reduce household tension in a country where nearly 35% of women report intimate partner violence—higher than the global average of 27%.

Typically, Liberia provides $250 annually to needy households, usually managed by women to improve economic control amid widespread gender inequality , explained Aurelius Butler, Liberia's national coordinator for social protection.
