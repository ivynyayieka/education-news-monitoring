Utkarsh Classes — 8 Oct 2026

India and Ghana have signed two MoUs to work together on agriculture and science, as announced on 7 October 2026. External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar and Ghana's Foreign Minister Samuel Okudzeto Ablakwa witnessed the exchange.

South-South cooperation means developing countries sharing knowledge and technology with each other. This MoU gives Indian institutions like ICAR a chance to take their research to African farms.

The ICAR deal runs for five years. It covers joint research, scientific exchange, capacity building and the commercial use of farm technologies. Arima Farms Ltd., Ghana, is the commercial partner to CSIR-CRI.
